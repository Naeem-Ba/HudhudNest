Self-hosted web fonts (QA phase 17, P17-D04). Roboto, IBM Plex Sans Arabic and Cairo, latin + arabic subsets,
from the @fontsource packages. Licences: Roboto (Apache-2.0), IBM Plex Sans Arabic (SIL OFL 1.1), Cairo (SIL OFL 1.1).
fonts.css declares the faces; index.html links it. Do not load fonts from fonts.googleapis.com again: it sends every
visitor's IP address to Google before they have agreed to anything.
