## Requires Legal Review

- Syrian governing law and its interaction with EU/Turkish consumer law if those markets are targeted.
- Whether the EU Digital Services Act notice-and-action and statement-of-reasons duties apply, and whether the e-mail
  appeal route is enough (no in-app appeal exists).
- Consumer dispute resolution statement (§ 36 VSBG) for German consumers.
- Fees section once payments are switched on (cancellation/withdrawal rights).
